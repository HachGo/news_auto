### 6. Everyone hates Oxford Street. Will pedestrianisation save it? {#i-9b029b6f29}

London's mayor has announced that Oxford Street, Europe's busiest shopping street, will close to traffic on 26 October, starting with half of its length. The question is whether pedestrianisation can turn a polluted, widely disliked thoroughfare into somewhere people actually want to spend time. The piece reports the plan's surprisingly real progress and examines the urban-governance logic behind it. Worth reading for what it reveals about how big cities remake their flagship shopping streets.
