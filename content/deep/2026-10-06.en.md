### 4. Astronomers catch dwarf stars gobbling up their orbiting companions {#i-49bfab57d2}

Astronomers have caught red and white dwarf stars devouring their orbiting companions, suggesting such stellar feeding may be more common than previously thought. The accreted material can alter a star's atmosphere and radiation, offering clues about stellar accretion and the fate of planetary systems. The finding challenges conventional models of dwarf-star evolution and accretion, making it worth reading in full.
