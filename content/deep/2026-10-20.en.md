### 3. Japan launches a probe to collect rocks from Mars’s moon Phobos {#i-c262dd5c4f}

Japan's Martian Moons Exploration (MMX) mission aims to land on Phobos and bring rocky samples back to Earth. Whether the Martian moons are captured asteroids or debris from a giant impact on Mars is still disputed, and the samples could settle the question. The probe will also study Deimos and test technologies for future Mars exploration. It is worth reading as the next ambitious sample-return effort after Hayabusa2.

<p class="deep-source"><a href="https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/japans-mission-to-collect-rocks-from-mars-moon-phobos-achieves-liftoff/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Scientific American</a></p>
