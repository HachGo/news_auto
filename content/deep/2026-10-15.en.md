### 3. Could AI help scientists with their paperwork? {#i-659756346a}

Scientific paperwork — grants, ethics reviews, compliance filings — consumes ever more of researchers' time, and AI tools promise relief by drafting, checking and filing documents. Worth reading as a caution that efficiency tools cannot substitute for reforming how science is administered and evaluated.
