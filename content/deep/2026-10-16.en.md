### 5. A perfect storm: how the super El Niño is devastating the fishing industry {#i-486a997576}

Peru’s anchoveta has accounted for roughly a tenth of the global commercial catch since 1970, more than any other fish. In June 2026, Peru suspended the anchoveta season, then turned it into a total closure in August. Warming seas and decades of overfishing created a perfect storm. The article explains the economic and supply-chain consequences, and why this case matters for global fisheries governance.
