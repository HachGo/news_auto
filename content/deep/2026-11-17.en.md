### 3. Neanderthal DNA linked to lower risk of schizophrenia in modern humans {#i-23d2b5a140}

It has long been known that anatomically modern humans and Neanderthals interbred, yet scientists are still working out how this legacy shapes modern genetics. New evidence links some Neanderthal DNA to a lower risk of schizophrenia in modern humans. The finding is worth reading because it connects human evolutionary history with the genetics of mental health and shows that archaic ancestry can have complex, disease-relevant effects.
