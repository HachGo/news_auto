### 1. Weirdly accelerating space rock keeps puzzling astronomers {#i-b27ba22f43}

The dark comet 1998 KY26 is moving in ways scientists cannot fully explain, and the anomaly remains unresolved. A Japanese probe was meant to visit it in 2031, but engine trouble has cancelled that encounter. Worth reading because the mystery touches basic questions about small bodies in the solar system and about planning future deep-space and planetary-defence missions.
