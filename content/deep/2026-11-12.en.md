### 3. Metal-rich deep-sea ‘nodules’ also harbor diverse ocean life {#i-00b94b6fe3}

Researchers report that the metal-rich polymetallic nodules scattered across the deep seafloor are not barren ground but host a diverse array of ocean life. The finding adds fresh ecological evidence to the debate over deep-sea mining. The authors say this biodiversity should factor into plans to mine the nodules. As commercial mining edges closer, the science behind these nodules deserves a closer look.
