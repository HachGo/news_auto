### 4. America’s grim record of failed executions {#i-34eb168b56}

Lethal injection is meant to be a single, final act, yet American states have repeatedly bungled it — missing veins, botched drugs, halted executions and second attempts. This piece catalogues those failures and asks how procedural negligence, medical ethics and state incompetence combine into needless cruelty. It is a sobering look at what the death penalty actually looks like in practice, essential reading for anyone weighing capital punishment and official accountability.
