### 3. Eli Lilly shows GLP-1 weight-loss pill Foundayo can dramatically improve sleep apnea {#i-774c67974f}

Eli Lilly reports that Foundayo, a once-daily GLP-1 weight-loss pill, significantly reduced obstructive sleep apnea in clinical trial data. The result could position the drug as a new medication option for OSA and broaden the use of weight-loss treatments. It is worth reading for efficacy details, safety, and regulatory implications.

### 4. The coin-flipping trick that counts the Internet {#i-92ced57697}

This article uses a coin-flipping analogy to explain HyperLogLog, a widely used probabilistic algorithm for estimating the number of distinct items in massive data streams. It shows how the method achieves Internet-scale cardinality counting with very little memory, balancing accuracy against resource use. Understanding it offers insight into how modern data systems handle the basic problem of distinct counting efficiently.

Europe’s busiest shopping street closed to traffic on Monday 26 October, as London hopes to turn a disliked and polluted thoroughfare into a place where people want to spend time.
